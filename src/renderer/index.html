    After a break

    A full-screen window, like the break window, appears when a break has ended and the next focus session still hasn't started. It waits while you are away from the computer.

    Idle detection

    While you are away the focus clock is frozen at the moment of your last keystroke, so idle time is never counted as work — even if the machine sleeps.
