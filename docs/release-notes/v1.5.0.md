## What's new in 1.5.0

**The history chart now has a vertical axis.** Ticks and gridlines, labelled in
hours (or pomodoros), so a bar's height is a number you can read. The axis always
rounds up to a sensible top — quarter, half and whole hours, never 2.5 of an hour
— and never cuts a bar off.

Above the chart: switch between **Hours focused** and **Pomodoros**, and between
**7, 14 and 30 days**. Each bar is labelled with its weekday and date, and today's
bar is outlined.

**Click any bar to open that day in full.** The popup shows:
- when the day started and ended, and how long it ran;
- focus time, pomodoros, focus sessions and the average session length;
- breaks actually taken, long ones counted separately, and time spent on them;
- idle time removed, interruptions, overtime and time added by hand;
- focus time by task;
- every session of that day with its outcome, each with a ✕ that removes it and
  unwinds the day's totals.

**← Previous day** and **Next day →** walk through your history without going back
to the chart. Esc closes the popup.

## Installing or updating

1. Download **Ferna-Pomoro-Setup-1.5.0.exe** below.
2. **Already have Ferna Pomoro?** Quit it first: right-click its tray icon (near
   the clock) and choose **Quit**. Closing the window only hides it.
3. Run the installer. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click
   **More info → Run anyway**. The installer isn't code-signed.
4. It updates the existing app in place. Your statistics, tasks and settings are
   kept, so you don't need to uninstall the old version first.
5. Check it: **Settings** should say **Ferna Pomoro 1.5.0** at the bottom.

Prefer not to install? **Ferna-Pomoro-Portable-1.5.0.exe** runs without
installing. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

To verify a download, compare it with `SHA256SUMS.txt`:
`Get-FileHash .\Ferna-Pomoro-Setup-1.5.0.exe -Algorithm SHA256`
